# GuanDan! — Learn & Play · v1 Design

**Date:** 2026-06-10 · **Status:** Approved by Yorick (interactive review)

## Product

An iOS game that teaches Western players Guandan (掼蛋) and lets them play full
matches against AI bots. The tutorial and first-session experience is the core
product, not an afterthought: 4 of the 10 screens are dedicated to learning.
English UI, App Store target outside China. v1 is fully offline — no backend,
no accounts. "Quick Match" and "Play with Friends" appear in the lobby as
Coming Soon.

Visual design source of truth: the 10 Stitch screens in
`~/claudecode/guandan-design/screens/` (PNG + HTML each). Design tokens: felt
teal `#0F4C46`, ivory cards, coral `#FF6B5E` primary, gold `#E8C268` accents,
Plus Jakarta Sans, 16px rounding, Duolingo-like friendliness.

## Rules (standard Jiangsu Guandan)

- 108 cards: two standard decks + 4 jokers (2 red "big", 2 black "small").
- 4 players, 2 teams, partners sit across. Both teams start at level 2 and
  race to win a hand while playing level Ace.
- Rank order within a hand: level cards rank just below jokers
  (… A < level card < small joker < big joker). Heart-suited level cards are
  wildcards (逢人配) substituting any card except jokers.
- Combos: single, pair, triple, full house (3+2), straight (5), tube
  (3 consecutive pairs), plate (2 consecutive triples), bomb (4–8 of a kind),
  straight flush, four jokers (highest).
- Bomb hierarchy: 4-bomb < 5-bomb < straight flush < 6-bomb < 7-bomb < 8-bomb
  < four jokers. Any bomb beats any non-bomb.
- A trick: leader plays any combo; others must beat it with the same shape
  (or bomb) or pass. Trick winner leads next. Hand ends when 3 players have
  emptied; finishing order decides level gain: partner finishes 2nd → +3,
  3rd → +2, 4th → +1.
- Tribute (进贡/还贡): last hand's loser(s) pay their highest non-wild card to
  the winner(s); receiver returns any card ≤ 10. Anti-tribute (抗贡) when
  payers hold both big jokers. Taught in Lesson 4; toggleable in Settings.
- Winning at Ace requires the Ace-level hand to be won with partner not
  finishing last; otherwise the team stays at A (simplified A-rule for v1).

## Architecture

Two layers, one Xcode workspace generated by xcodegen:

**GuandanCore** — local Swift Package, zero UI dependencies, fully unit-tested.
- `Card`, `Rank`, `Suit`, `Deck` — identity-stable cards (two decks ⇒ each
  card has a copy index).
- `Combo` — detection (`Combo.allPlayable(from:level:)`) and comparison
  (`beats(_:level:)`), wildcard expansion for heart level cards.
- `GameEngine` — deal → tribute phase → tricks → scoring → level update, a
  pure state machine: `apply(action) -> GameState` with legality validation.
  Every state transition is data, so the UI and tutorial replay it.
- `Bot` — protocol + `HeuristicBot` (shape retention, partner awareness,
  bomb discipline; 3 difficulty levels by widening the candidate search).
- `MatchSession` — drives a full match of hands until a team wins at Ace.

**GuandanApp** — SwiftUI, iOS 17+, mirrors the 10 Stitch screens.
- `DesignSystem/` — colors, typography, card views, buttons from the tokens.
- `Screens/` — Welcome, Onboarding, Lobby, GameTable, RoundResult, Profile.
- `Tutorial/` — `TutorialScript` (declarative steps: stacked deck, spotlight
  target, coach text, allowed actions) + `TutorialRunner` overlaying
  GameTable. 5 lessons: What is GuanDan → Card Combos → Your First Hand →
  Tribute & Climbing → Bombs & Strategy.
- `Hints/` — practice mode asks the strongest bot for its move and explains it.
- `Progress/` — lesson completion, match stats, achievements in SwiftData.

## Error handling & testing

- Engine rejects illegal actions by returning typed errors; UI prevents them
  upstream, so an engine error is a bug — assert in debug, no-op in release.
- GuandanCore: XCTest unit tests for combo detection/comparison (incl. wildcard
  edge cases), trick legality, tribute, scoring; plus a fuzz test playing
  thousands of random bot matches asserting invariants (108 cards conserved,
  hands terminate, no illegal plays).
- App layer: snapshot-light — manual QA against the Stitch PNGs.

## Milestones

1. Core engine (cards, combos, tricks, scoring) + tests
2. Bots + headless full-match loop + fuzz tests
3. Design system + Welcome/Onboarding/Lobby/Profile shells
4. GameTable interaction (select, play, pass, bot turns, animations)
5. Tutorial engine + 5 lessons + practice hints
6. RoundResult, achievements, app icon, polish, TestFlight via fastlane

## Revision 2 (2026-06-10, Yorick): landscape + tutorial-first

1. **Landscape only.** 27-card hands don't fit portrait. All screens redesigned
   for landscape; hand fan spans the bottom, coach panel docks right.
2. **Tutorial-first product.** The journey home IS the app home. Curriculum:
   - Basics (5): What is GuanDan · Card Combos · Your First Hand ·
     Climb to Ace · Bombs!
   - Techniques (5): Partner Play (scripted) · Wildcard Mastery ·
     Bomb Timing · Card Counting · Endgame Lines (scripted)
3. **AI matches serve learning.** Every hand vs bots is recorded
   (initial deal + action list) and can be REVIEWED (复盘): step through every
   play with all hands face-up; where the human's move differs from the hard
   bot's choice, show the coach's alternative. Practice Arena reached from the
   journey home.
